About My Diary & Writing

How does 16-line page writing work?

My Diary is crafted like a real physical notebook. Each page is limited to exactly 16 lines. When you reach the 16th line and press Enter or keep typing, your writing is saved automatically and a fresh new page opens smoothly.

How do I navigate between pages?

You can easily flip through past and future pages using the "← Previous" and "Next →" buttons at the bottom. Your notes are saved before each navigation so nothing is ever lost.

Can I add audio voice notes, stickers, and pictures?

Yes! Tap the "● Voice" button to record audio notes into your diary, click "▧ Stickers" for expressive journaling, or choose "＋ Add media" to attach photos and videos to your memory.

Is my writing automatically saved?

Yes, everything you write is saved in real-time as you type. A subtle "Saved" indicator at the top right of your page gives you peace of mind.

Security & Customization

Privacy, Backups & Themes

Is my diary private and secure?

Yes! Every thought, photo, voice recording, and memory is strictly private to your own account. No one else has access to your journal.

How can I download or backup my diary?

Open the Settings tab where you can download an offline JSON backup or export a beautifully stylized PDF backup of all your pages. You can also import any previous JSON backup file anytime.

Can I change the themes and fonts?

Yes! You can choose between Day, Night, Cinematic, Pink, and Classic themes in Settings, alongside your preferred handwriting or book-style font.

How do Categories work?

Categories allow you to organize your diary entries into distinct life chapters (like Travel, Ideas, Memories, Personal). You can assign custom symbols and colors to each category.
